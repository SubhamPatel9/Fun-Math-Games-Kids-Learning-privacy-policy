Official Privacy Policy

Fun Math Games: Kids Learning

Package ID: com.orinexa.math.games.kids.addition.subtraction.saga
Effective Date: November 10, 2026

1. Overview

Welcome to Fun Math Games: Kids Learning ("the App"), developed by Orinexa. We are committed to protecting the privacy of children, students, parents, and players of all ages. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play our offline-first math puzzle and brain-training game.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Fun Math Games: Kids Learning does not require users to create an account or sign in. We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), including:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • Precise GPS or device location data
  • Photos, camera feeds, microphone audio, or contacts

3. Local On-Device Game Data

All gameplay progress is stored strictly locally on your device using Android's internal storage (Room SQLite database and SharedPreferences):

  • Level Progress & Stars: Unlocked levels (1–500), star ratings, and daily streak counters.
  • In-Game Currency & Timers: Earned bonus coins, achievement states, and the 7-hour mystery chest timer.
  • App Preferences: Sound effects, background music, and haptic feedback toggles.

You can erase and reset all locally stored game data at any time using the Reset All Progress button inside the in-app Settings screen or by uninstalling the App.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display family-safe banner ads, natural-break interstitial ads, and optional user-initiated rewarded video ads (such as unlocking bonus chest coins, extra lives, or hints).

  • AdMob may use non-personal device identifiers and coarse network status solely for frequency capping, fraud prevention, and serving contextual advertisements.
  • Rewarded video ads are completely optional and only play when explicitly initiated by the player.
  • For more details on how Google processes data, please review Google's Privacy & Terms.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Our game is designed to be educational and safe for kids and families. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal information has been inadvertently provided, please contact us so we can immediately remove it.

6. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used only to load optional advertisements when an internet connection is available. The core 500-level math game works 100% offline.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to the App or regulatory requirements. Any updates will be included within the App and on our official website.
